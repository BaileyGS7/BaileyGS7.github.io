Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

Trend Shorts Bot ("the app") is a personal automation tool run by its owner (GitHub user BaileyGS7) for the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what it does with it.

Data the app accesses from TikTok

Only from the owner's own TikTok account, and only after the owner authorises it through TikTok's login screen:

  • Basic profile information: open ID, display name and avatar. Used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos, used to send the owner's own videos to the account as drafts or posts.
  • The owner's video list and its public statistics: video IDs, titles or captions, and view, like, comment and share counts. Used to measure which videos perform well.

The app does not access other users' accounts, messages, followers' personal data or any data beyond the above.

How data is stored

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never written to logs or files.
  • Video IDs, captions and statistics are stored in a private GitHub repository owned by the operator.
  • Generated videos are kept temporarily as private workflow files and are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Services used to make videos

To create videos, the app uses Anthropic (AI script writing and web research), Pexels and Pixabay (stock footage), Microsoft text-to-speech or ElevenLabs (voiceover) and GitHub (hosting and automation). Titles and view counts of the owner's own past videos may be sent to Anthropic's API to help write better scripts. No other TikTok data is shared with these services.

Sharing and selling

The app does not sell, rent or share TikTok data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions. Revoking access stops the app immediately. The stored statistics are deleted when the operator deletes the repository, or on request.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
