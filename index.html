Trend Shorts Bot

A personal tool that turns what's trending into short explainer videos and publishes them to its owner's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. It checks public trend lists (such as TikTok Creative Center and Google Trends) for topics people are talking about.
  2. An AI writer researches a safe, suitable topic and writes a short voiceover script.
  3. The video is assembled from a computer-generated voiceover, licensed stock footage and on-screen captions.
  4. The finished video is uploaded to the owner's TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API, either as a draft for the owner to review or as a post. Every video is labelled as AI-generated.

Who it's for

Trend Shorts Bot is operated by one person for their own TikTok account. It is not a public service and does not access anyone else's account or data.

TikTok permissions it uses

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): confirms which account is connected.
  • Upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish): sends the owner's own videos to their account.
  • Video list (video.list): reads view, like, comment and share counts on the owner's own videos so future videos can improve.
