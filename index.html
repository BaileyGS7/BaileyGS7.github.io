Trend Shorts Bot

A personal tool that turns what's trending into short explainer videos for its owner's own TikTok account and own YouTube channel.

How it works

  1. It checks public trend lists (Google Trends and Reddit) for topics people are talking about, or searches the web for them when those lists give nothing usable.
  2. An AI writer picks a safe, suitable topic, usually after researching it on the web, and writes a short voiceover script.
  3. The video is assembled from a computer-generated voiceover, licensed stock footage and on-screen captions.
  4. The finished video goes only to the owner's own accounts, and each platform can be switched on or off separately:
    • YouTube: uploaded through the YouTube Data API as a private Short, marked as altered or synthetic content. The owner watches each one in YouTube Studio and decides whether to publish it.
    • TikTok: uploaded through TikTok's official Content Posting API, either as a draft for the owner to finish and post in the TikTok app or as a post. Videos are labelled as AI-generated (the owner switches the label on for drafts).
  5. With the owner's permission, it reads the view counts and watch-time statistics of the owner's own YouTube videos, to learn which of its video formats viewers watch for longest.

Who it's for

Trend Shorts Bot is a personal tool operated by one person for their own TikTok account and their own YouTube channel. It is not a public service, only the owner can connect an account to it, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.

TikTok permissions it uses

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): confirms which account is connected.
  • Upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish): sends the owner's own videos to their account.

YouTube permissions it uses

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): uploads the owner's own videos to their own channel as Shorts.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): reads the view counts and watch-time statistics of the owner's own videos, to learn which of its video formats work.

Nothing else is read from the Google account. How the data is used, stored and deleted is explained in the Privacy Policy.
