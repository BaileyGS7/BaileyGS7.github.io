1. What the app is

Trend Shorts Bot ("the app") is a personal tool, run by its owner (GitHub user BaileyGS7), that creates short videos and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official APIs, and to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not a service offered to the public, and only the owner may connect a TikTok account or YouTube channel to it. Publishing to TikTok and to YouTube can each be switched on or off separately.

2. Content rules

  • All content published through the app must follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, and YouTube's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • Videos are made from AI-written scripts, synthetic (text-to-speech) voiceovers and licensed stock footage. They are labelled as AI-generated on TikTok (the owner switches the label on for drafts) and as altered or synthetic content on YouTube, where every description also says that the script is written by AI and read by a synthetic voice.
  • The app is designed to avoid sensitive topics such as tragedies, violence, crime, politics, medical or financial advice and anything involving minors, as well as shopping deals, giveaways and product promotion.
  • Footage, music and fonts are used only under licences that allow it: the Pixabay and Pexels content licences for footage; for optional background music, only YouTube Audio Library tracks marked "Attribution not required" or public-domain (CC0) tracks; fonts under free licences that allow it (the SIL Open Font License and the DejaVu fonts licence). Sound effects are generated by the app itself.

3. YouTube

The app uploads the owner's videos to their own YouTube channel using YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of data received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Videos are uploaded only to the owner's own channel, with the privacy status the owner chooses in the app's settings. At present every video is uploaded as private: the owner watches it in YouTube Studio and decides whether to publish it, keep it private or delete it.
  • Titles and descriptions are written by the app, unless the owner types their own when starting a run. The owner can change them in YouTube Studio before publishing a video, or at any time after.
  • Every upload is marked as altered or synthetic content and as not made for kids.
  • With the owner's permission, the app also reads the view and watch-time statistics of the owner's own videos to learn which of its video formats work. See the Privacy Policy.
  • By uploading through the app, the owner certifies that each video complies with the YouTube Terms of Service, including the YouTube Community Guidelines, and that it doesn't violate others' copyright or privacy rights. When the owner starts a run, the app's run form asks for this with a box that must be ticked before anything is uploaded; for scheduled uploads, the owner agrees to it when connecting the channel.

4. Account access

TikTok: the app acts on a TikTok account only after the account owner authorises it on TikTok's login screen. Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services permissions.

YouTube: the app acts on a YouTube channel only after the channel owner authorises it on Google's sign-in screen. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, or with the app's Disconnect YouTube step, which revokes the access with Google and deletes the saved login and the stored YouTube data.

See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
