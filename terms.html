1. What the app is

Trend Shorts Bot ("the app") is a personal tool, run by its owner (GitHub user BaileyGS7), that creates short videos and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official APIs, and to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not a service offered to the public, and only the owner may connect a TikTok account to it.

2. Content rules

  • All content published through the app must follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.
  • Videos are made from AI-written scripts, synthetic voiceovers and licensed stock footage, and are labelled as AI-generated on TikTok.
  • The app is designed to avoid sensitive topics such as tragedies, violence, politics, medical or financial advice and anything involving minors.
  • Footage and music are used only under licences that allow it, such as the Pexels and Pixabay licences.

3. YouTube

The app also uploads the owner's videos to their own YouTube channel using YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Uploaded videos are marked as containing altered or synthetic content where YouTube requires it.

4. Account access

The app acts on a TikTok account only after the account owner authorises it through TikTok's login screen. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok's settings. See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
